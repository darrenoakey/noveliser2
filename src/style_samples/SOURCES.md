# Calibration samples for prose_checks

- `human_*`: roughly 1500-word passages from public-domain US novels via Project Gutenberg
  (ebook 64317 Fitzgerald, *The Great Gatsby*; 67138 Hemingway, *The Sun Also Rises*;
  541 Wharton, *The Age of Innocence*), sampled at random by paragraph.
- `model_*`: scenes previously generated by noveliser2 (the old prompt set) and
  stored under `output/*/chapter_*_section_*.json`, sampled at random.
- `legacy_writing_style.json`: a `writing_style.json` checkpoint written before the
  richer style fields existed, used to prove old checkpoints still load and render.

The review triggers in `prose_checks.LIMITS` sit above the rates in the human passages
and below most model scenes. They are engineering heuristics, not research results
and not an authorship detector.
- `legacy_characters.json`, `legacy_outline.md`: the same book's cast and the opening of its outline.
